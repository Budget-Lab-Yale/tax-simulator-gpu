# The compiled tax law schema

Generated by `src/taxmodel/law/schema_doc.py` from the frozen baseline compile in `tests/golden/fixtures/law/`. Do not edit by hand.

## Shape

Every field is a `float64` array indexed `[year, filing_status, slot]`, shaped `(12, 4, 16)` for this compile.

- Years: 2025–2036 (12 of them). The parser
  reads from 2014 regardless, because carry-forward and indexation both look at
  years the run does not simulate.
- Filing status, in order: 1 single, 2 married filing jointly, 3 married filing separately, 4 head of household.
- Slots: 16 (`MAX_BRACKETS`). A schedule shorter than that leaves the
  remaining slots inactive; a law needing more is a lint error, not a reshape.
- Padding is `NaN`, and every field ships an `active` mask of the same shape.
  Inactive means the law has nothing there: a slot past the end of the schedule,
  or a year before the first one the author stated a value for.

## Reading the columns

| column | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| units | what the numbers are denominated in: dollars, a rate, a share of AGI, a count of people or years, a calendar year, a regime code, or an on/off indicator. |
| slots | how many slots the law fills in this compile. One means the field is a scalar and its `tax_law.csv` column carries no index. |
| by FS | whether the filing-status mapper gives the four statuses different values. A field that does not vary is still stored four times. |
| indexed | whether the law grows this field with a price or wage index. |
| mapper | whether the field is built by a filing-status mapper from other subparameters of its file, rather than stated directly. |
| grad | whether the field belongs in the differentiable parameter vector. Switches, counts, calendar years and regime codes do not; neither do individual infinite entries, which say a limit does not bind. |
